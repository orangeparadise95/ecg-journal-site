Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes ECG Journal for Polar H10, the support mailbox, and this support website. The app is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Polar Electro. Privacy questions can be sent to ecgjournal.help@outlook.com.

Your ECG is processed and stored on your iPhone. The app does not automatically send recordings or analysis results to the developer. Sharing an export, contacting support, and visiting this website are separate actions described below.

1. Information used on your phone

With Bluetooth permission, the app connects to your Polar H10 through the Polar BLE SDK. It receives ECG samples, sensor and receive timestamps, heart rate, battery information, and device information. Recording files include device identifiers/names and session metadata. The app also stores recording coverage, your polarity annotations, experimental analysis checkpoints and reports, and app preferences.

This information is used for live display, local recording, replay, annotations, and optional on-device analysis. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or developer analytics. Its recording and analysis features do not upload this information to a developer server or to a Polar cloud service.

2. Storage, protection, and retention

Recordings and associated analysis data are kept in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. The recording directory is excluded from automatic backup. Files use iOS protection available after the first device unlock, allowing recording while the phone is locked; this does not make the data inaccessible to every person or process with access to an unlocked device.

Recording, analysis, and export operations can create local working files or copies. Completed exports can remain in the app's storage. Export data you need before uninstalling the app. No developer-operated cloud copy is available to restore your recordings.

3. Sharing that you choose

Exports can contain sensitive raw ECG, timestamps, device/session metadata, annotations, and analysis reports. Data leaves the app when you choose a destination in the system share sheet, such as Files, Mail, or another app. The selected service or recipient handles that copy under its own terms and privacy practices. Share only with recipients you trust. Deleting data here does not delete copies already exported elsewhere.

4. Your controls

You can stop recording, revoke Bluetooth permission in iPhone Settings, export your data, or delete daily recordings. A day being recorded is protected from deletion until capture stops. Day deletion also removes its saved analysis jobs and reports. Removing the app removes its local app data but not copies you have shared outside it.

The developer cannot access or remotely delete recordings stored only on your phone. Contact support for help exercising these controls or for questions about personal information you sent to support.

5. Support email

If you email support, your email address and the contents of your message are received in an Outlook mailbox. They are used to respond to your inquiry and handle support or privacy requests, not to provide medical care. Messages remain in the mailbox until deleted; you can request deletion by emailing the same address. Please do not send raw ECG, medical records, passwords, or unnecessary identifying information.

Email is processed by Microsoft and your email provider. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for Microsoft's practices.

6. This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. Visiting this site does not upload your app's ECG data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Experimental use and policy updates

The app is not a diagnosis or emergency monitor. Consult a doctor before medical decisions. If data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. English and Simplified Chinese versions are provided; contact support if any wording is unclear.
