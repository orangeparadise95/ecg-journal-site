Get started

  1. Use an iPhone running iOS 17 or later and a Polar H10 chest strap, sold separately.
  2. Wear the strap with moistened electrodes. Allow Bluetooth access when prompted.
  3. In Live ECG, scan for the H10 and select your device. Keep it nearby.
  4. Open Recordings for daily coverage and replay. Stop recording before analyzing a day in PVC Analysis.

Where are my recordings?

ECG recordings, annotations, and analysis reports are stored in the app on your phone. There is no app account or developer-hosted recording service. The recording directory is excluded from automatic backup. Export anything you want to keep before deleting the app.

How do export and deletion work?

Recordings are grouped by Los Angeles dates, not your phone's current time zone. Daily export creates a ZIP containing raw recording segments, annotations, saved count reports, and a manifest. Segments are grouped by their first receive time, so boundary samples may cross a date.

Delete an entire day only after reviewing the confirmation. Today's recordings and any day with an open segment are protected during capture; other historical days can be exported or deleted. Deleting a day also removes its saved analysis state and reports. Copies previously shared outside the app are not deleted.

Why did the signal stop?

Check strap contact, battery, Bluetooth permission, distance, and other apps using the sensor. Reconnection is manual. Background recording can be interrupted by force quit, process termination, or restart; uninterrupted recording is not guaranteed. About contains connection diagnostics.

What do the analysis results mean?

Results are experimental model candidates, not confirmed diagnoses. Personalized-model results do not establish accuracy for other people. A 24-hour estimate assumes the same candidate rate during unrecorded or excluded time; it is not a measured full-day count. Keep the app foregrounded while counting. Paused work can resume if the recording, annotation, and model inputs still match.
